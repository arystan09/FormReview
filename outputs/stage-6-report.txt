Known limitations
Unsaved edits are kept in the open tab, not durably cached. Browser crash, deliberate
reload/discard or closing the tab can lose unsaved content; navigation warnings are
provided. Manual reconciliation requires the reviewer to compare all fields; there
is no automatic merge. Source anchoring verifies exact membership, not supplier
truth or government registration. Approved documents cannot start another review.
Public demo remains read-only/keyless; no publication occurred. Stage-5 gold review
is still pending, test extraction remains untouched, and no live/generalization
claims are made. Stages 7–10 remain unimplemented.
